## Thread, 4 posts

> 1/ Robot arms are good at picking things up and bad at using tools.
>
> So for the @AIatMeta hackathon I built Bevell 3D: photograph a tool, get grippers made for it. 🧵

> 2/ Put the scissors on a printed sheet and follow the guided camera. The markers give every photo its scale and position.
>
> Muse Spark plans the scan, SAM 3.1 finds the scissors in each photo, SAM 3D builds the whole shape.

> 3/ Then it designs new jaw parts for the SO-101. It finds the screw the scissors turn on and puts it on the gripper's hinge, so the blades open as far as the jaw: 25.8° and 25.8° in MuJoCo.
>
> Each finger loop snaps into its own clip.

> 4/ Unscrew the stock jaw, screw on the new parts, snap the scissors in.
>
> First prototype, scissors only for now. It also makes measured CAD and printable holders for anything else you photograph.
